# Research Question
Can a low-cost double-axis solar tracker increase the net energy collected by a small solar panel compared with a stationary solar panel?
# Independent Variable
Tracking vs. stationary panel
# Dependent Variables
- Electrical power produced
- Electrical energy collected
- Energy consumed by tracker
- Net energy benefit
# Control
Stationary Panel





# Constraints
- 5W solar panel
- Single-axis tracking
- Arduino controlled
- Automatic
- Low-cost
- Portable
- Target cost: ~$75 or less
